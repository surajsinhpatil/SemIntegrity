## 1. The six schema axes

§4.3 asks for 6-8 schemas per class, each instantiated 4-5 times, *and* 6
families per domain, *and* one constraint per class per family. Those counts
close only if family coincides with (domain, schema index). So one axis
defines, at once, a family theme in each of the 5 domains and a schema in
each of the 4 classes: 6 axes → 30 families → 24 schemas → 120 constraints.

| # | axis | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | the invariant it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Unique designated role-bearer** | `entity` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `entity` | A thing has exactly one designated bearer of a named functional role, and the role's answer space is closed. |
| 2 | **Disjoint category membership** | `boolean` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `boolean` | Two categories are mutually exclusive by definition, so membership of one entails non-membership of the other. |
| 3 | **Part-whole containment** | `entity` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `entity` | A containment hierarchy in which each part sits in exactly one whole at a given level, and containment composes. |
| 4 | **Event precedence** | `date` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `date` | Two dated events stand in a fixed order, and the order is derivable from the dates without naming them. |
| 5 | **Extremal uniqueness** | `boolean` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `boolean` | A superlative over a closed comparison set has exactly one bearer, and the comparison set must be stated to make it well-defined. |
| 6 | **Interval boundary** | `date` | `boolean` | `boolean` | `date` | An entity or arrangement has a terminating boundary date, and nothing it did may be placed outside its interval. |

**Answer type is a function of (axis, class)** — ADR-0017 as amended
2026-10-07. C2 and C3 are `boolean` on every axis: a conjunction and a
universal have no single binary subject-relation-object, and the approved
probe generator routes a direct+affirmative probe of an entity/date-answered
constraint through `_realise_open`, which requires exactly that. C1 and C4
retain the axis-fixed allocation. The allocation of CELLS is untouched.

chance-PSCR is 1 - 1/*m*, where *m* is the closed answer space's size: the
contradiction rate two independent draws would produce before any model
behaviour is involved. Under the amended rule:

| | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chance-PSCR | 0.611 | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.611 |

**Confirmatory contrast C1+C2 vs C3+C4: 0.556 vs 0.556, gap
0.000** — exactly balanced, and §4.11 rests the claim
on that contrast. The four-class **descriptive** profile carries a
0.111 spread, against 0.000 under the original
axis-only rule and 0.167 under a fully class-tied one. Answer type enters
the secondary model as a **covariate**, never as an experimental factor.
This allocation is an **interpretation** of §4.3's "comparable answer
type", not a requirement of it; see ADR-0017 and
`docs/DESIGN_RESOLUTION_2026-10-06.md` §3.

**D0 scope.** Two behaviours are implemented and two are decidable at all:

* **`EXCL`** — closed-space mutual exclusion. `c1_mutual_exclusion` documents
  itself as *schema-general*, carries no facts and uses no knowledge base.
  Over a closed, mutually exclusive answer space — which §4.7 requires of
  every constraint — contradiction *is* "the answers differ". Covers 24/24.
* **`ANCHOR`** — temporal anchor precedence. The only predicate that decides
  something `EXCL` cannot: a *single* response preceding
  `temporal_validity.start`, even when both responses agree. Needs a
  parseable date answer, so it reaches 4/24 schemas under this allocation.
* **`N/A-struct`** (12/24, every C2 and C3 schema) — the class's structure is
  not decidable from a Track A response. The probe asks the conjunction or
  the instantiated claim as a whole and the model returns one token, so a
  model that checked one conjunct is indistinguishable from one that checked
  both, and nothing in the response carries the quantifier or its variable
  binding. A design fact, not a missing implementation.

So the minimum predicate scope is **no new detection logic**: 24 thin
per-schema registrations over the two existing behaviours, each carrying its
own `schema_id`, `version` and `formal_statement` so §4.8.2's three-instance
audit is per-schema and the freeze manifest records what each does *not*
formalise. D0 coverage per class is a reported result in its own right
(§4.8.1), so stating the limit is the requirement, not a concession.

## 8. Decision record

All four methodology items below were settled on 2026-10-06. They are kept
here, with their evidence, because the allocation above is unreadable without
knowing which readings of §4.3 and §4.8 it rests on.

### M1 — ADR-0002 and ADR-0003 (APPROVED 2026-10-06)

This matrix is an instance of ADR-0002's lattice, and the 960 probes it
implies are an instance of ADR-0003's factor balancing. Both were approved
on 2026-10-06, which is what makes authoring against this allocation safe:
rejection after authoring would have re-keyed all 120 records.

### M2 — answer type: an interpretation, not a requirement (DECIDED)

§4.3 asks families to hold nuisance variation **approximately constant**
across classes, listing *"comparable answer type"* among them. Its words
are "approximately" and "comparable", not "identical" — and
`answer_type` is not one of §4.3's 17 per-constraint fields at all. It is a
*probe* field in §4.4.1, and §4.7 positively expects it to vary within a
constraint, since directionality "mixes 'Is A the X of B?' (yes/no) with
'What is the X of B?' (an entity)". §4.4.2 reserves "balanced exactly
across classes" for the four probe transformation factors, which do not
include answer type.

The axis-fixed allocation above therefore **satisfies** §4.3 but is not
**required** by it. The 12 pilot constraints tie answer type to class
instead (C1 `entity`, C2/C3 `boolean`, C4 `date`), which is a perfect
functional dependence and harder to defend as "approximately constant
across classes" — but it is a deviation from a soft requirement, not a
violation of a hard one. §1 above gives the quantified difference. Two
consequences of the axis-fixed choice are real costs:

* `ANCHOR` reaches only the `date` axes, so D0's structural coverage on C4
  is 10/30 under the approved two-date-axis allocation, against 30/30 had
  answer type stayed tied to class and 5/30 with a single date axis;
* the 12 pilot constraints stop being instances of these 24 schemas, so
  ADR-0007's "dev constraints reuse the **same schemas**" needs re-wording
  or 12 dev constraints that do match.

**Decided 2026-10-06 (A3 + A4):** axis-fixed answer type with answer-space
cardinality retained as a covariate, and two `date` axes. Recorded in
`docs/decisions/ADR-0017-answer-type-allocation.md`. The three-way
comparison is in `docs/DESIGN_RESOLUTION_2026-10-06.md` §3.

### M3 — the accounting (CONFIRMED 2026-10-06, A7)

ADR-0007 makes the 120 the test set and sends the 12 pilot constraints into a
separate 24-constraint dev set, disjoint from the 120. The authoring job is
therefore **120 new test constraints** — not 108 — plus 12 more dev
constraints to bring dev to 24: **144 at freeze, 132 newly authored**, and
**1,056 new primary probes** (960 test + 96 new dev; the existing 96 pilot
probes stay as pilot/dev material). See `docs/BENCHMARK_ACCOUNTING.md`.

### M4 — D0 predicate scope (DECIDED)

The minimum scope is **no new detection logic** — see §2's D0 note. What
needs deciding is whether to accept that limit or change what the probes
ask. Genuine C2 and C3 predicates are not constructible from a Track A
response; making them constructible would mean probing conjuncts or
quantifier instances separately, or admitting knowledge-screen answers into
D0 — and §4.11 makes prerequisite conditioning "a reporting view, never an
inclusion criterion". Either route is a redesign, not an implementation
task. Accepting the limit costs nothing but honesty in the write-up, since
§4.8.1 already treats D0 coverage as a result.

### E1 — index allocation (engineering, proposed as taken)

Test constraints and families take index **11..16**; 01..10 stays with
pilot/dev. Section 7 shows the alternative collides on all 12 existing ids,
and renumbering the pilot instead would desynchronise the two byte-identical
pilot result files that ADR-0005 makes immutable.
