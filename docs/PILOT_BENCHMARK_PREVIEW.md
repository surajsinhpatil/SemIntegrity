### Structure (ADR-0002)

**3 families × 4 classes = 12.** A family is fixed to one domain and contributes
exactly one constraint per class — the proposal's nuisance-control device (§4.3).

* classes: C1 3 · C2 3 · C3 3 · C4 3 — **exactly 3 each, as §4.12 requires**
* domains: geography 4 · public_office 4 · science_technology 4
* **history and biology are NOT covered.** 12 divides by 4 classes but not by 5
  domains. Three complete families was preferred over five partial ones, because
  family completeness is the structure Phase 1 exists to test. Recorded, not hidden.

## The 96 probes

8 probes per constraint, generated by the existing factor generator
(`probes/factor_design.py`) and realised by `probes/surface.py`.

Every constraint carries each `(polarity × register × directionality)` cell
**exactly once** — 4 affirmative / 4 negated, 4 formal / 4 colloquial, 4 direct /
4 reverse. Syntax splits 3/3/2, because 8 is not divisible by 3; balance is a
class-level property (ADR-0003). Across the pilot all **24** factor cells appear.

| Probe | Cell | Expects | Text |

| P05 | inte / neg / formal / direct | NO | Is it false that the capital city of Australia is Canberra? |
| P06 | cloz / neg / formal / reverse | NO | It is not the case that Canberra is the capital city of Australia: ___ |
| P07 | decl / neg / colloq / direct | NO | It is not the case that the capital of Australia is Canberra — YES or NO? |
| P08 | inte / neg / colloq / reverse | NO | Is it false that Canberra is the capital of Australia? |

**Q21 is visible here and is deliberately not resolved.** P01 expects an entity;
P05–P08 expect `NO`. Whether those are probes of *the same* question — and so
whether this constraint yields 28 comparable pairs or two separate sets of 4 —
depends on `EQUIVALENCE_KEY_FIELDS` in `schemas/probe.py`, which currently
excludes `polarity`. Both polarities are generated and each probe records its own
expected answer, so either decision works on exactly this data. **Yours to make.**

**Q22 is likewise open.** Both D0 verdicts (`check_pair` and `check_set`) are
implemented and will both be computed; which one the paper calls "D0" is undecided.

### On K = 5 vs K = 8

§4.12 specifies "12 constraints × 5 probes" for the pilot *run*. The factor
design's balance guarantees only hold at K = 8, so the **authored** set is 8 and
each probe records `pilot_run_subset=yes` for the first 5. The 5-probe subset is
**not** factor-balanced; that is a property of §4.12's budget, documented rather
than worked around.

## What still requires human review

Nothing below can be resolved by a script.

1. **Every constraint, individually.** Wording, class assignment, answer-space
   distractors, and whether the gold answer is genuinely unambiguous.
2. **Two recorded source discrepancies.** Wikidata gives the inception of the
   office of President of the United States (`Q11696 P571`) as **1787** (the
   Constitution signed), while `K-C1-POL-01` and `K-C4-POL-01` concern **1789**
   (Constitution in effect; first holder took office). Both years are recorded
   as evidence. **These constraints are blocked from EVIDENCE_VERIFIED until you
   adjudicate**, even though every check passes — a check that confirms a
   discrepancy exists does not resolve it.
3. **C3 class assignment.** `K-C3-GEO-01` and `K-C3-SCI-01` instantiate a
   universal on a single entity, so a model can answer correctly without
   representing the quantifier. Whether these are genuinely C3 rather than C1 is
   a judgement call.
4. **`K-C3-POL-01` is not machine-verifiable.** Its evidence confirms only that
   President and Vice President are distinct offices. The universal claim rests
   on constitutional knowledge, and the *simultaneity* qualifier is load-bearing
   — several people have held both offices at different times.
5. **C2 predicates are placeholders.** `c2_conjunction` and
   `c3_functional_uniqueness` check mutual exclusion over the closed answer space
   only; they do **not** verify the conjuncts separately or represent the
   quantifier. Their `formal_statement` says so and it is copied into every audit
   record. Genuine schema-specific predicates are Phase 5 work.
6. **Family coherence in F-SCI-01.** It mixes chemical elements with a scientific
   award. Entity familiarity is comparable (gold 277, Nobel Prize 114) but the
   family is less coherent than §4.3 intends.
7. **Probe semantic equivalence.** §4.4.5 requires two blinded annotators per
   probe. 96 probes × 2 = **192 judgements, none performed.**

### A limitation the pilot surfaced

**Class is perfectly correlated with answer type**: C1 → entity, C2/C3 → boolean,
C4 → date. In the 120-constraint benchmark this would confound any class effect
with answer format. Answer type must be varied *within* class. A test asserts
this correlation so that the note cannot silently go stale.

## Why nothing was promoted to EVIDENCE_VERIFIED

You asked to see the candidates and the evidence before any promotion. The build
therefore ran with evidence fetching **on** and promotion **off**: every record
carries its machine-checked evidence and every record says `DRAFT`.

To promote the ten constraints that carry no recorded discrepancy:

```bash
python scripts/benchmark/build_pilot.py --fetch-evidence --promote --force
```

The two ⚠ constraints will stay DRAFT regardless until the 1787/1789 question is
adjudicated.
